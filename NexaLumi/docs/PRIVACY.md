# Privacy assumptions

Legal review is required before public launch, including Indian data-protection requirements.

## What this prototype collects

Only what the visitor types into demo forms, kept in that browser:

- Display name and email for the demo session
- Scan metadata (not full message bodies)

Nothing is sent to NEXALUMI servers or third-party AI/threat providers in this phase.

## User control

Delete history in Settings, or clear site data in the browser.
